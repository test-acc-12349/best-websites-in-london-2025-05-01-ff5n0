Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom website?

Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks. Simple websites may take 2-3 weeks, while complex e-commerce platforms can take 8-12 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What's included in your website maintenance packages?

Our maintenance packages include regular updates, security monitoring, daily backups, performance optimization, technical support, and monthly analytics reports. We offer flexible plans to suit different business needs.

Do you provide SEO optimization?

Yes, all our websites are built with SEO best practices in mind. We include on-page optimization, mobile optimization, schema markup, and speed optimization. Additional advanced SEO services are available.

What is your pricing structure?

Our pricing is project-based and depends on your specific requirements. Basic business websites start from £2,500, while e-commerce solutions start from £5,000. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your needs.

Can you help with content creation?

Yes, we offer professional copywriting and content creation services. Our team can help develop engaging website content, blog posts, and product descriptions that align with your brand voice and SEO strategy.
